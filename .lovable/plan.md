# Editable goals + deadline-aware, spread-out planning

## 1. Edit a goal from the dashboard

- Each goal card on the dashboard gets an edit (pencil) button next to its title, matching the one already on schedule items.
- It opens the same Create Goal form, pre-filled, in "edit" mode: title, description, category, check-in frequency, target date, status.
- Saving updates the goal in place; the dashboard, planner and roadmap all refresh straight away.
- The form also gets a Delete option, with a confirm step.

## 2. Goal work spread out, not in one stretch

Today all pending steps get packed into the earliest free gaps, so several land back-to-back on the same day.

New behaviour:
- At most one goal block per goal per day, so two goals can share a day but one goal never eats the whole day.
- A cap of 2 goal blocks per day overall (about 2 hours), instead of the current 4-hour lump.
- Blocks are spaced out across the days you actually have room, rather than filling the first free day.
- A short gap is kept between a goal block and the commitment that follows it, so nothing feels squeezed.

## 3. Nothing scheduled past the goal deadline

- Steps and milestones are only placed on days up to the goal's target date; if a goal has no date, current behaviour is unchanged.
- Steps for a goal are spread evenly between today and its deadline instead of all at the front.
- When the remaining days can't hold all remaining steps, the goal card shows a plain notice ("3 steps won't fit before your deadline") with a shortcut to extend the date or reduce steps.
- Milestone due dates generated by the AI suggestion flow are clamped to the goal's target date, and the periods are divided across the time available instead of using fixed 7/14/30-day steps.

## Technical notes

- `useUpdateGoal` and `useDeleteGoal` already exist in `src/hooks/useGoals.ts`; `CreateGoalDialog` gains an optional `editGoal` prop and switches mutation accordingly.
- `src/lib/timeAllocation.ts`: `allocateTasks` gains `maxBlocksPerDay`, `maxPerGoalPerDay`, `bufferMinutes` and a per-task `notAfter` date; allocation iterates day-by-day round-robin over goals rather than task-by-task over slots.
- `useAllActiveGoalSteps` also selects `target_date` per goal so `useTimeAllocation` can pass `notAfter`; unplaceable steps are returned as `unscheduled` for the dashboard notice.
- `useMilestones.ts`: `dueDateFor` derives its step from the span between today and `goal.target_date`, divided by the number of periods, and never exceeds the target date.
- No database changes.
